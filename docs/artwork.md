The CRT screen is deep black and displays a striking glowing amber/orange wireframe latitude-longitude globe, horizontal phosphor scanlines. Full computer fills most of the image with very little empty space. Balanced designer-made tactile two-ink risograph art, torn print zine graphics, obsessively precise drawing. No words or letters or logo or typography, no header, no border, no UI mockup, no gradient. This is artwork of a workstation, not an actual product screenshot.
